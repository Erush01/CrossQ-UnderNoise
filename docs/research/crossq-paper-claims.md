# CrossQ Paper-Claim Audit

**Date:** 2026-10-26  
**Purpose:** Check what the CrossQ paper and implementation sources state, and distinguish those source-level findings from claims independently validated by this project. This is an evidence audit, not a full experimental reproduction.

## Summary

- The core method claims (BRN, a joint current/next critic pass, and removing target networks) are supported by the paper and reflected in the checked SB3-Contrib 2.9.0 implementation.
- The paper reports that CrossQ at UTD 1 achieves competitive sample efficiency against methods such as REDQ and DroQ at UTD 20. That is a **paper-reported empirical result**, not a result independently established by the local pilot.
- Five Hopper-v4 5M policies (seed0 reused; seeds1–4 trained) are now strictly validated and summarized at the training-seed level. A separate HalfCheetah-v4 seed0 5M run remains outside the latest proposal. None of these runs has a matched baseline arm; they cannot validate the paper's REDQ/DroQ comparison or six-task/ten-seed results. The separate HumanoidStandup-v4 100k run remains a resource/stability pilot, not a 5M performance result.
- The latest proposal supersedes the earlier three-task × three-seed plan: its target is Hopper-v4, seeds 0–4, 5M steps each, followed by test-time action-noise evaluation. Hopper seed0 is reused and seeds1–4 are complete. Seed3 attempts1 and2 stopped at75k and1.675M respectively and are excluded. The 300-episode action-noise evaluation is complete; its output is `CrossQ/results/proposal_hopper/action_noise.json`.
- SB3-Contrib's default critic width is `[1024, 1024]`; this project explicitly configures `[2048, 2048]` to match the paper's reported critic width. The actor width `[256, 256]` is a modern SB3 default, not a paper-specified width.

## Latest proposal alignment — CrossQ Under Action Noise

`CrossQ/docs/crossq.pdf` proposes a scaled robustness study on Hopper-v4: five training seeds (0–4), 5M steps each, followed by evaluation of each final policy at action-noise levels σ∈{0, 0.05, 0.10}. The extension specifies the same held-out reset seeds 90000–90019 for every condition, matched standard-normal noise streams for the nonzero levels, action clipping, and 300 total episodes. The current completed Hopper seed-0 run is reusable as the seed-0 training result because its actual task, seed, 5M horizon, Table 1 mappings, and evaluation cadence match; its saved manifest still lists the earlier three-task/three-seed scope and must be disclosed. The HalfCheetah run is outside this proposal's analysis and remains preserved but excluded.

The seed-0 metadata also records hashes for the old `scaled_reproduction_jobs.py` and `run_scaled_reproduction.sh` files. Those two orchestration files changed later, and their byte-for-byte historical snapshots were not preserved. The original completion validation passed; current proposal validation rechecks the unchanged core training sources and emits explicit warnings for the two legacy launcher hashes. Keep this provenance limitation visible if reusing seed 0.

The proposal's 95% bootstrap interval differs from Figure 5's reported 70% quantile intervals. The completed action-noise summary retains the proposal's 95% intervals, resampled over five training seeds; these must not be conflated with the paper's 70% interval. The proposed ±20% endpoint tolerance is marked “subject to approval” and was not applied. The action-noise evaluation completed 300/300 cases with finite metrics; σ0.05's paired-delta interval includes zero, while σ0.10's interval is below zero. These results are limited to this five-seed Hopper evaluation.

### Nominal no-noise endpoint versus the paper

In the user-provided `CrossQ/1902.05605v4.pdf`, printed page 5, Figure 5's Hopper panel places the CrossQ curve at 5M steps at approximately **1.9–2.1k IQM return** by visual reading; the paper does not tabulate an exact endpoint. The local five-seed training-final IQM is **2,080.95** (proposal 95% bootstrap CI **[1,992.68, 2,202.60]**), so the point estimate is consistent with the paper curve's approximate value. This is agreement in magnitude, not an exact or statistical-equivalence claim. The local held-out σ=0 evaluation mean (2,076.84 over resets 90000–90019) is similarly close but is a separate evaluation protocol, not the primary Figure 5 comparator. Figure 5's caption specifies IQM with 70% quantile intervals over 10 seeds; locally there are five seeds and the proposal reports a 95% bootstrap interval. The official experiments also use the authors' JAX implementation, whereas this project uses SB3-Contrib 2.9.0/PyTorch with its documented modern evaluation choices. The proposal's ±20% criterion remains unapproved and was not applied.

## Overall verdict

**Source-level method claims:** supported by the official paper and checked CrossQ implementation, with the actor-width and SB3-default distinctions above.  
**Paper-reported empirical claims:** the cited paper reports them, but they are not independently validated by this project's experiment.  
**Local claim supported:** the configured SB3-Contrib CrossQ method completed five Hopper-v4 5M policies under the current scaled proposal, including the reused seed0, and a 300-episode paired action-noise evaluation of their final checkpoints. The separate HalfCheetah seed0 5M run remains outside this proposal, and HumanoidStandup seed0 remains a 100k resource/stability pilot. There is no local REDQ/DroQ/SAC comparison. The ±20% success criterion remains unapproved and was not applied.
